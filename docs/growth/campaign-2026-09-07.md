## Opening post — published

**Free Shopify inventory health check: what should you reorder first?**

Skubase is offering a free inventory health check for three Shopify merchants who handle purchasing and are unsure which products to reorder first.

The check focuses on a small set of repeat-selling SKUs: stock on hand, recent sales, supplier lead time and any incoming stock with a known arrival date. The goal is a short list of reorder priorities, slow-moving stock to review, and assumptions worth checking—not automatic purchasing or a promise of perfect forecasts.

A useful first check: compare how many days your available stock is likely to cover with the time it takes a supplier to deliver. Incoming stock only helps if it arrives before you run out. Seasonal products and recent stockouts need extra care because recent sales can misrepresent demand.
