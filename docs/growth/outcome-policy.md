# Customer outcomes operating policy, September 14, 2026

The newest owner instruction makes paying Skubase customers and MRR the north star.
The prior 1,000-contact objective is retained as historical owner direction and
available capacity, not the definition of success or a reason to repeat a failing
approach. Contacts are inputs. Never report volume alone as marketing success.

## Evidence and attribution

Use the existing first-contact ledger, immutable cohort snapshot, message/reply
records, authenticated prospect/shop links, and verified product/billing events.
Track observed stages independently; a later stage does not invent earlier ones.
UNKNOWN is not zero. A provider-accepted email is a confirmed contact, not proof
of delivery. A confirmed form receipt is a submission, not proof someone read it.
Keep attempted, confirmed, uncertain, failed, bounced and each channel separate.
Never retry an uncertain contact to determine whether it was sent.

Rank signals: paid, checkout/explicit purchase intent, trial, connected Shopify,
completed inventory analysis, positive/access request, substantive response,
account, qualified visit, confirmed contact, impression/discovery. Higher outcomes
dominate activity. Historical Reddit membership interest is one
PURCHASE_INTENT_EVIDENCE observation, not a purchase or payment.

New first-contact cohort labels: icp, industry (UNKNOWN permitted), Shopify
confidence, source, channel/contact_method, message_variant, offer, positioning,
cta, message_version and experiment_id. Store them with exact message and receipt.
Never change old cohort labels to make an experiment appear consistent.

## Controlled learning

Primary offer hypothesis: free Shopify inventory health check. Ask for a small
response about seeing what Skubase finds; demonstrate value before asking to buy.
Use meaningful motivations, not endless wording variants:
A overstock/trapped cash; B stockout/reorder risk; C inventory health check;
D inventory decision automation; E replacing manual spreadsheet work.
Only make verified capability claims. Assign a stable variant before contact.
Do not pretend existing unspecified historical messages belonged to these variants.

Use 25 contacts as early signal, 50 as review, 100 as stronger decision guidance,
not statistical certainty. Allow response time and preserve cohorts. Approximately
75–100 relevant confirmed/delivered contacts with essentially no substantive
responses warrants changing target/message/offer/channel before hundreds more
using the same approach. A small zero-reply cohort does not establish no demand.
Strong positive results, severe bounce/opt-out evidence can justify earlier action.

## Autonomous decisions

Read acquisition_outcomes and the durable acquisition review before planning.
Diagnose contacts→responses (target/message/offer/channel/delivery), responses→
positive (value/ICP/problem/CTA), positive→signup (landing/trust/friction), signup→
connection (onboarding/auth), connection→activation (import/time-to-value/analysis),
activation→payment (value/pricing/purchase CTA), paid→churn (ongoing value).
Choose the next experiment from the observed break. Never infer a downstream
failure from missing attribution. Customer/product issues become structured
feedback, not unreviewed major production changes or blanket acquisition holds.
At each approximately 100-contact checkpoint retain a compact outcome review;
meaningful evidence changes update beliefs with source IDs, sample size,
contradictions, confidence and date. Deterministic aggregation requires no LLM.
Keep the useful channels running and let the planner replenish work itself.

## Owner reporting

Lead with customers/MRR, then trials, connections, positive and substantive
responses, then confirmed contacts. Show today, last seven days, all time and
current experiment separately. Include best signal, bottleneck, hypothesis and
next decision/action. Report known observations and attribution gaps explicitly.
Costs without a complete allocation remain UNKNOWN; no invented zero CAC or MRR.
Do not describe a full queue, many sends or a passing test as validated demand.
