# Safe product identity and clearer first steps

Synthetic review found two ways SKU-only lookups could mix products: current variants sharing a SKU, and an old CSV record preceding a single current product. The former could give different histories to forecast detail and list views, while the latter could hide a current product's sales. This release keeps supplier-facing SKU text and historical records intact, and adds a conservative resolution rule rather than migrating product identity.

An alias resolves to a single product with recorded inventory, including zero on hand. When there are no such current products, a unique historical product remains resolvable. Multiple current candidates require review. Raw codes, truncated compatibility IDs and generated aliases are checked together within the merchant's shop.

Products requiring review remain visible with their own recorded stock and sales. Forecast and action responses carry explicit identity and availability metadata; purchasing, clearance, bundles and incoming-stock allocation cannot select an arbitrary variant. Reports explain excluded products and unknown complete estimates. Actual inventory and revenue aggregates remain separate from withheld planning estimates. Merchants are directed to review source mappings and assign distinct codes to distinct variants before syncing again.

Lead-time and purchase-order writes preflight aliases before mutation and return a clear conflict for unsafe changes. Unchanged legacy lines can remain while unrelated safe work continues. Preserved lead-time overrides are disclosed rather than silently cleared. Receipt requests cannot lose duplicate lines through dictionary conversion. Historical product records, saved orders and receipts are not merged or deleted by this release.

Empty dashboards and unavailable connection screens now offer the public browser inventory health check directly. Its copy names the required summary columns and explains that results are not imported into the account. Stocky current-stock imports and explicitly non-Shopify shipment history remain distinct from approved Shopify order sync while the App Store listing is in review.

Verification passed 331 backend tests, followed by 25 focused tests after the final planning-availability correction, and 215 frontend tests. Typecheck and the production build passed. The final correction marks existing no-history monitoring tasks as unavailable for planning, so their compatibility zeros cannot acquire a new known-value meaning. Receipt overages and empty receipt submissions now reject before mutation; scheduled action emails use REVIEW and Unknown for held plans.

Forecast, purchasing and analytics stayed within a 390px viewport. Transfer empty states also explain source-data requirements without assuming that an empty result proves only aggregate inventory was imported.
