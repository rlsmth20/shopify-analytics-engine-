1. PRODUCT

Skubase (https://skubase.io) is a Shopify-focused inventory planning and purchasing
app. Older files call it Inventory Command or Shopify Inventory Decision Engine.
Those are historical names for this repository, not separate products.

The central value proposition is helping merchants decide what to reorder and
turn those recommendations into exportable purchase orders. The app also has
inventory health, forecasting, supplier, reporting, and operational workflows.
Keep the experience understandable to a merchant, with clear next actions and
honest explanations of missing data. Do not invent stock, cost, revenue, forecast
confidence, or product capabilities when underlying evidence is absent.

Current code surfaces include:
- Dashboard and prioritized Action Queue.
- Inventory health/analytics, demand forecasts, and lead-time settings.
- Purchase orders, exports, receipt workflows, and stock transfers.
- Supplier views, bundle opportunities, and liquidation/dead-stock views.
- Reports and spreadsheet exports, scheduled reports, alerts and notifications.
- Shopify connection/sync, Stocky migration/import, and ShipStation import.
- Account, billing, support/feedback, and privacy requests.
- Ask Skubase inventory assistant with controlled model access and local fallback.
- Public pricing, features, blog, inventory-health-check and calculator pages.
- Separate administrative growth/outreach functionality.

Presence of a page is not proof that every underlying workflow is production
verified. Confirm behavior and plan entitlement before promising a feature.
Skubase DOES support exportable purchase orders.

Read AGENTS.md first. Its key requirements are:
- Do not modify backend logic unless explicitly requested.
- Keep frontend/backend separate; communicate through HTTP contracts.
- Never introduce runtime imports across frontend/backend boundaries.

Inventory action flow:
  UI -> HTTP client -> FastAPI actions route -> services/action_feed.py ->
  services/inventory_engine.py -> ranked inventory recommendations.
Persisted shop/product/inventory/order data and shop settings underpin the feed.
Demo/mock data exists; never present it as a merchant's verified live inventory.

Authentication includes server-side sessions and a standalone magic-link flow,
plus embedded Shopify authentication. Do not replace the Shopify review testing
instructions with obsolete magic-link-only instructions.
Preserve tenant/shop isolation, session boundaries, and cache protections.
Do not expose unverified paid entitlements when billing checks fail.

Ask Skubase is evidence-bounded and has spending/access gates, no external tools
or inventory mutations, and an honest deterministic fallback. Do not remove
those gates or introduce paid API usage to work around another service's limit.
