# Shopify Community: after Stocky

Updated September 9, 2026. Contextual draft, not a scheduled or published post.
Use only when it answers the actual merchant question and the thread rules allow it.

## Response starting point

For your next reorder, separate three jobs: deciding quantities, preparing the supplier order, and receiving stock. Save your Stocky reports while read-only exports remain available, then test those jobs with a small group of products in your replacement workflow.

Shopify's migration guide covers its current inventory and purchasing workflows:
https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/inventory/transitioning-from-stocky

If the question is specifically about reorder planning or exportable POs, an optional relevant disclosure:

I work on Skubase. It uses recent sales, stock on hand, and supplier lead-time settings to prioritize reorders. You can turn those recommendations into supplier-grouped PO drafts and export an Excel workbook. Skubase is in Shopify's review process and is not yet listed in the App Store; CSV workspaces and a free inventory health check are available now.
